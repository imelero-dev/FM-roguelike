⚽ RELEGATIONRUN

a football management roguelike

One season. One shot. Win the league or vanish.

How to play

  • 🏆 Goal: win an 8-team league over 14 matchdays.
  • 💀 Game over: 3 defeats in a row, or not finishing top.
  • 🃏 Cards: your squad is a deck of cards with 33 attributes on a 1-20 scale. Tap a card's magnifier to see the full profile.
  • 📋 Tactics: pick from 7 formations and set mentality, pressing, passing style and width. Every opponent has its own plan: check the scout report and counter it.
  • ✨ Synergies: field players sharing a tag (Academy, Superstar, Physical, Technical) to unlock team bonuses.
  • 🧠 Staff: up to 3 staff cards with run-wide effects.
  • ⚡ Fitness and form: playing tires players; rotate the squad or injuries will follow. Winning runs boost morale and form.
  • 💰 Economy: gold for wins, wages every matchday, and negotiated transfers in and out.
  • 🔥 Boss matches (matchdays 5, 10 and 14): buffed opponents, guaranteed high-rarity loot.
